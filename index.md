This project looked at how to present geometric ideas in ways that suitable for a wider audience.
In particular, we focused on ways to generate visual depictions of the homologous generators of the space of specific Seifert fiberings.
The results displayed on this site are largely outlined in this [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.08545).
